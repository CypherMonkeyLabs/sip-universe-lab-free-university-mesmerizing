Early media and 183

183 Session Progress with SDP is the usual early-media pattern. 180 means ringing. A 183 does not always have SDP.

Takeaways

  • Recognize 183 with SDP as a usual early-media pattern
  • Treat 180 as ringing
  • Read a 183 that has no SDP as still a 183

Learn in 5 minutes

  2. Treat 180 as ringing

Worked example

183 has SDP, 180 does not Extract both responses. One progress message has SDP and the ringing message does not.

  1. Press Extract SIP.
  2. Open the 183 and look for m=.
  3. Open the 180 and confirm there is no SDP.

Fault

The caller plays local ringback and a remote announcement at the same time.

RFC 3960 separates remote early media from local ringback. Playing both is the clipping and double-audio failure.

Glossary, sister systems, and the integration seam for this room.

AI omni-channel agents A voice bot still needs a session. SIP creates, modifies, and ends sessions. Omni-channel, in the contact-center sense, means the same contact can also live on chat or another digital channel. Those channels do not replace the voice session. On Amazon Connect the platform owns the managed PSTN voice channel. Bridging an outside carrier into Connect with a Chime Voice Connector is not a supported pattern unless AWS reviews it.
