CUCM, a certified SBC, and Teams Direct Routing

CUBE or another certified SBC can sit between CUCM and Teams. No architecture SKU is invented here.

Takeaways

  • Place a certified SBC between CUCM and Teams
  • Require TLS 1.2+ and an FQDN Contact
  • Keep the SIP target on sip.pstnhub.microsoft.com

Ecosystem

Glossary, sister systems, and the integration seam for this room.

AI omni-channel agents A voice bot still needs a session. SIP creates, modifies, and ends sessions. Omni-channel, in the contact-center sense, means the same contact can also live on chat or another digital channel. Those channels do not replace the voice session. On Amazon Connect the platform owns the managed PSTN voice channel. Bridging an outside carrier into Connect with a Chime Voice Connector is not a supported pattern unless AWS reviews it. When an SBC forks the voice leg to a recorder, that fork is SIPREC, a different plane from the customer dialog.
