What the RFCs actually cover

RFC 3261 defines digest authentication for SIP. TLS protects a hop, not a diagram. SRTP (RFC 3711) protects media; the keying can be SDES (RFC 4568) or DTLS-SRTP (RFC 5764). They do not interoperate by hope. STIR (RFC 8224) puts a PASSporT in Identity. SHAKEN is the ATIS profile North American carriers use. Attestation letters are policy. Confirm the current ATIS specification and the FCC call-authentication page before you treat a letter as a rule.

Toll fraud defense, as vendors document it, is a trusted source list and a dial plan that does not allow the world to place outbound calls through you. Recording consent and emergency policy are legal questions for the place you operate. This page is not that advice.
