PSTN and BYOC options

Options you can actually tell apart

A premises SIP trunk lands on your SBC. You own the certificate, the dial plan toward the carrier, and the media anchor unless the carrier’s design says otherwise. Bring-your-own-carrier on a cloud contact center means the cloud publishes a trunk target and you present calls to it. Genesys documents BYOC Cloud and BYOC Premises. Five9 documents BYO SIP. Twilio documents Elastic SIP Trunking. NICE publishes a port list and leaves carrier design account-specific. Read the page for the product you turned on.

Teams Phone has more than one PSTN door: Calling Plan, Operator Connect, Teams Phone Mobile, and Direct Routing through a certified SBC. Microsoft’s planning page says those can coexist in one tenant. Webex Calling separates a Local Gateway from Cloud Connected PSTN. They are different location settings. Amazon Connect’s own telephony is the supported PSTN door; bridging an arbitrary carrier with a Chime Voice Connector is not a pattern AWS documents as generally available.
